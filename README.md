### Section: Results (Shown after calculation)

Your Projected Returns

**If Long–Term Rental is selected:**

**Subheading:**
Long–Term Rental Projection

* **Total Investment (₦):**
  Property Purchase Price + Renovation & Fit–Out Costs + Other Acquisition Costs

* **Gross Annual Rental Income (₦):**
  Monthly Rental Income × 12

* **Annual Operating Expenses (₦):**
  Property Tax + Insurance + Maintenance + Management Fee (calculated from gross income)

* **Net Annual Income (₦):**
  Gross Annual Rental Income – Annual Operating Expenses

* **Cap Rate (%):**
  Net Annual Income ÷ Property Purchase Price × 100

* **Cash–on–Cash Return (%):**
  Net Annual Income ÷ Total Investment × 100

* **Estimated Payback Period (Years):**
  Total Investment ÷ Net Annual Income

**Body text:**
Before a property appears on Therizo, we:

**Confirm Ownership and Title**
We verify ownership and title status with our legal partners and relevant authorities, and we request supporting documentation from the seller or developer.

**Check Approvals and Compliance**
Where applicable, we review planning permissions, building approvals, and estate regulations to understand what is allowed and what has been done.

**Test the Price Against the Market**
We look at comparable sales, rental levels, and demand in the area. If the numbers do not make sense, we say so and we either renegotiate or walk away.

**Assess Risk and Exit Options**
We consider vacancy risk, resale possibilities, and likely holding period. We prefer assets where a buyer has more than one realistic exit.

# COMPANY STRUCTURE, VISION, MISSION & STAFF MODUS OPERANDI (THERIZO)

1. **Company Snapshot**

**Name:** Therizo Real Estate & Development Ltd
**Focus:** Buying, selling, and structuring high–quality real estate deals in Nigeria, with clean documentation and clear returns for serious buyers and investors.

---

2. **Vision**

To be the most trusted real estate and development partner for serious buyers and investors in Nigeria, known for clean title, disciplined numbers, and projects that actually get completed.

---

3. **Mission**

To source, vet, and structure Nigerian property deals that protect capital and create long–term value for clients, using strict due diligence, simple communication, and accountable execution.

---

4. **Core Company Structure (Year 1)**

**1. CEO – Victor**

* Sets strategy, markets, and client focus.
* Approves all properties Therizo will touch.
* Leads capital, partnerships, and key negotiations.

**2. Senior Property Consultants – Bayo, Kelly, Aisha**

* Each owns a defined territory and portfolio of listings.
* Responsible for leads, viewings, offers, and closed deals.
* Direct point of contact for clients from first enquiry to completion.

**3. External Partners (Not staff but essential)**

* Legal: title checks, contracts, approvals.
* Surveyors / Valuers: land surveys, valuation, physical checks.
* Developers / Landowners: supply side and JV opportunities.

Everyone is either bringing deals, protecting deals, or closing deals. No “passengers”.

---

5. **Modus Operandi – How Staff Are Expected to Work**

Keep this simple and non–negotiable.

### A. How We Handle Properties

No listing goes live without basic checks:

* Ownership and title documents seen and logged.
* Basic sanity check on price vs. market.
* Any red flags recorded and discussed with Victor.

We prefer fewer, stronger listings.

If a property looks messy on paper or the seller is evasive, we walk away.

---

### B. How We Handle Clients

**Speed + Clarity.**

* All client calls and WhatsApp messages acknowledged same day where possible.
* If you don’t know the answer, say “I’ll confirm and get back to you,” then actually do it.

**No hype, no lies.**

* We never promise returns or approvals we cannot back up.
* We share both strengths and weaknesses of a property.

**Document everything.**

* Calls, viewings, offers, and decisions are logged in the CRM.
* If it is not written, it didn’t happen.

---

### C. Daily Work Rhythm for Senior Property Consultants

Each SPC is expected to, every week:

* Generate new leads (calls, referrals, social, offline).
* Book and conduct viewings.
* Push active deals toward a decision (yes or no).
* Update the CRM properly once per day (not once per month).

**Minimum discipline (guide, not fluff):**

**Daily:**

* Outreach to prospects.
* Follow–up messages/calls on existing leads.
* CRM update (new leads, status changes).

**Weekly:**

Send a short pipeline summary to Victor:

* New leads this week
* Viewings done
* Offers on the table
* Deals likely to close in the next 30–60 days

---

### D. Decision Rules

**Property Acceptance:**
Victor has final say on what the company will list or promote.

**Price & Terms Strategy:**
Agreed between Victor and the SPC handling that deal; no one freelances major changes without alignment.

**Client Disputes / Problems:**
Escalate early to Victor with facts and documents, not stories.

Everyone is judged by results, not by how busy they look.

If an SPC consistently:

If someone:

# THERIZO REMUNERATION STRUCTURE

Here’s a clean structure you can drop into your **Therizo Staff Handbook**.

---

## 1. Principles

* **Revenue first, comfort second.**
  If the company is not making money, nobody should be comfortable.

* **Producers get paid. Passengers don’t.**
  The people who bring in and close business earn the most.

* **Simple and transparent.**
  Everyone must be able to calculate roughly what they’ll earn on a deal.

---

## 2. Key Definitions

* **Gross Commission** = Total commission received from a deal (e.g., 3% of sale price).

* **Direct Deal Costs** = Marketing for that property (ads, listing fees), referral fees to third parties.

* **Net Company Commission** = Gross Commission – Direct Deal Costs.
  This is what Therizo has left to pay staff and keep as profit.

---

## 3. Founder / CEO (Victor)

### 3.1. Equity

Victor holds 70–80% equity in Therizo.

Equity gives rights to dividends when the company is profitable.

### 3.2. Director’s Loan

Any cash Victor injects beyond his equity stake is documented as a Director’s Loan.

**Repayment priority:**

1. Operating expenses (rent, stipends, basic marketing)
2. Director’s Loan + agreed interest (e.g., 10–15% per year)
3. Profit retention and dividends

### 3.3. Salary

For the first phase, no or minimal salary until:

* The company has consistent net profit for at least 3–6 months.

After that, Victor may take a modest monthly salary (e.g., ₦300k–₦500k), reviewed annually.

### 3.4. Profit

After expenses and Director’s Loan repayment, profits are split:

* Portion retained in the business (e.g., 50–70%)
* Portion paid out as dividends to shareholders (Victor gets the largest share).

---

## 4. Senior Property Consultants (Bayo, Kelly, Aisha)

Each SPC is paid on two levers:

* Monthly Stipend
* Deal Commission

### 4.1. Monthly Stipend

**Standard Stipend:**

Example: ₦200,000 per month per SPC (you can adjust this later).

This is support, not a full salary.

It continues only if the consultant is active and moving toward targets.

**Stipend Review Rules:**

If an SPC goes 2 consecutive quarters with:

* Very low activity, and
* No meaningful closed revenue,

then the stipend can be:

* Reduced, or
* Stopped, moving them to commission–only.

No results, no guaranteed monthly comfort.

---

### 4.2. Commission on Deals

Commission is calculated on **Net Company Commission**, not gross.

**Base Structure:**

* If the SPC originates and closes the deal:
  **25% of Net Company Commission**

* If the SPC assists only (did not originate the client):
  **10–15% of Net Company Commission**
  (You can fix this at **12.5%** to keep it simple.)

**Example:**

* Sale Price = ₦150,000,000
* Gross Commission (3%) = ₦4,500,000
* Direct Deal Costs (ads, referral) = ₦500,000

Net Company Commission = ₦4,000,000

If Bayo originated and closed:

* Bayo gets 25% of ₦4,000,000 = **₦1,000,000**

Therizo keeps ₦3,000,000 (overheads + profit).

If Bayo and Aisha both worked on the deal and agree 60/40 split on the SPC share:

* SPC pool = 25% of ₦4,000,000 = ₦1,000,000
* Bayo: 60% of 1,000,000 = **₦600,000**
* Aisha: 40% of 1,000,000 = **₦400,000**

---

### 4.3. Performance Accelerator (Optional but Recommended)

To reward real producers:

If an SPC’s total **quarterly Net Company Commission** contribution is:

* Above ₦10,000,000:

  Commission on additional deals that quarter can be increased to **30%**.

If below target for 2 quarters:

* Stipend and role are reviewed.

This ensures top performers are heavily rewarded without fake titles.

---

## 5. Future Support / Admin Roles (When You Hire Them)

For non–sales staff (e.g., admin, operations):

* **Fixed Monthly Salary** (no commission).
* Optional small annual bonus tied to:

  * Overall company profit, and
  * Quality of support (measured by error rate, response times, etc.).

Keep them out of commission structures so incentives stay clean:
Salespeople sell. Support people support.

---

## 6. Summary (What Everyone Needs to Understand)

* Victor’s upside = equity + director’s loan repayment + profit.
* SPC’s upside = stipend + commission per deal + accelerator for high performers.

If deals don’t close, everyone feels it.

If deals close and margins are strong, everyone earns well, and the company grows.
